# Goal status and metric bridge

Use this reference to connect delivery work to goal measures and to make review decisions concise.

## Status definitions

| Status | Meaning | Required next step |
|---|---|---|
| Green | Current evidence supports the planned path | Continue and name the next dated work |
| Yellow | Risk exists, but a credible recovery path is available | Name the risk, recovery action, owner, and date |
| Red | Current evidence does not support the planned path | Escalate the decision, owner, and date |
| Complete | target met by the due date | Record the final measure and source |
| Complete late | target met after the due date | Record the final measure, date, and lesson |
| Did not meet | Period closed without the target | Record the final measure and authorized next decision |

Do not infer a status when current evidence or the planned trajectory is missing. Use `Status cannot be determined` and state the exact input needed.

## Metric bridge

Map work to an expected signal without claiming causation that the input does not support.

| Delivery item | Owner | Due date | Completion evidence | Lead measure effect | Lag measure effect | Basis |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| [Work] | [Owner needed] | [Date needed] | [Artifact or result] | [Expected change] | [Expected change] | [Evidence | Hypothesis] |

The bridge should reveal missing logic. If a delivery item has no plausible connection to a measure, revise or remove it rather than hiding the gap.

## Concise status update

Use this order:

1. status and current result against plan;
2. up to three important completed items;
3. up to three next dated items;
4. risk or blocker;
5. recovery action, owner, and date;
6. decision needed, owner, and date.

Report movement against the goal, not every task completed. Preserve the source of truth and date for each reported result.

## Review decisions

A review should produce one of four decisions:

- **Continue:** evidence supports the current plan.
- **Recover:** keep the goal and execute a dated recovery action.
- **Revise:** an authorized owner changes a target, scope, measure, or date and records why.
- **Close:** record complete, complete late, or did not meet with final evidence.

Never change the target or due date silently to turn a miss into success.
